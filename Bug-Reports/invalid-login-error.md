## Title
Error message overlaps Login button on mobile devices

---

## Severity
Medium

---

## Priority
Medium

---

## Environment
- Android 13
- Chrome Mobile Browser
- Resolution: 390x844

---

## Preconditions
User is on the Login page.

---

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the Login page on a mobile device.
2. Enter invalid email and password.
3. Tap the "Login" button.

---

## Expected Result
The error message is displayed correctly above the form without affecting UI elements.

---

## Actual Result
The error message overlaps the Login button, making the button partially hidden.
